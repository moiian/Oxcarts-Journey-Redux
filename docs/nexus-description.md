# Oxcarts Journey Redux

**Take the reins. Bring your companions. Make the journey part of the adventure.**

Oxcarts are more than a way to skip the road. Settle in with your traveling companions and watch the world go by—or take the driver's seat and guide the cart yourself.

## Your cart. Your journey.

- **Drive it yourself.** Take the driver's seat, steer along the road, and choose your pace: stop, walk, run, or dash.
- **Bring the whole company.** Seat up to **nine companions**, including Pawns and NPCs traveling with you. Give everyone a place and enjoy the scenery together.
- **Sit back and travel faster.** Ride as a passenger with automatic dash and destination slowdown, or adjust the cart's pace yourself.
- **Travel in style.** Seven carefully arranged seating presets cover normal, rainproof, and luxury oxcarts. Cycle through layouts, choose supported seated idles, or create your own arrangements.
- **Find your perfect view.** Customize seating positions and save separate passenger and driver camera settings, including field of view and distance. Your seating arrangements remain visible in Photo Mode.
- **Keep the journey going.** Added oxcart protection helps keep your cart intact through roadside trouble.

## Easy to make your own

Pay the fare and ride as usual, or approach the front of the cart and hold your **Let me drive** control to take the wheel. While riding as a passenger, the in-game skill bar displays your travel commands.

Press **Insert** and open **Oxcarts Journey Redux** to customize controls, seating arrangements, companion poses, and cameras.

Updating preserves your existing presets and keybinds. To try the included layouts, select **Restore all built-in layouts**; this resets the built-in arrangements while keeping layouts you created separately.

## Install

Requires **REFramework**. Install the ZIP with **Fluffy Mod Manager**, or merge the included `reframework` folder into your Dragon's Dogma 2 game folder. Restart the game or use **Reset scripts** after installing.
